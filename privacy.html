Privacy notice

How GreyByte handles initial enquiries during this website launch phase.

Draft notice — review and approve before publishing. This page describes the limited email-enquiry workflow currently offered by this website. It should be updated when secure application forms or a candidate database are introduced.

Information we receive

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message. This website does not currently provide account registration or CV uploads.

Why we use it

We use enquiries to respond to recruitment, employment and partnership questions and to assess whether a follow-up conversation is appropriate.

Sharing and retention

We do not publish enquiry contents on the website. Access should be limited to authorised staff and service providers needed to operate business email. Retention periods and internal handling procedures must be confirmed by GreyByte before this notice is treated as final.

Your choices

You may contact info@greybyte.co.za to ask about information you have sent and request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable law.

Security reminder

Do not send identity documents, financial details or sensitive personal information through an initial email enquiry. We will confirm a secure method where required.
